About Surya Bird Net Services

Safety Engineered Into Every Knot

Surya Bird Net Services is a Prayagraj-based safety net provider, dedicated to protecting homes, balconies and buildings with strong, weather-resistant netting. From bird control to fall-prevention nets, we treat every installation as if it were protecting our own family — with careful measurement, tight anchoring and a clean, professional finish.

  • Safety first. Every net is fitted and tensioned to trusted safety standards, with secure anchor points that hold firm through wind and rain.
  • Built to last. We use UV-stabilised, weather-resistant netting designed to withstand sun, rain and daily wear.
  • Always available. Open every day from 9:00 AM to 10:00 PM, so you can book a visit at a time that suits you.
  • Customer satisfaction. 22+ happy customers and a perfect 5-star rating reflect the trust we've earned across Prayagraj.
